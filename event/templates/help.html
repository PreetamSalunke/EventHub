Getting Started & Basic Usage

Account Management

  • How to Register/Sign Up: Click on the "Sign Up" button, usually found in the top right corner, and follow the simple steps to create your account using your email address.
  • How to Log In/Log Out: Use the "Log In" button to access your account. To log out, click on your profile/username in the navigation bar and select "Log Out."
  • Password Reset: If you've forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it.
  • Updating Profile: (If applicable) You can usually update your personal details and preferences from your user dashboard or profile settings.

Browse Events

  • Searching for Events: Utilize the search bar to find events by keywords, title, or description.
  • Filtering Events: Refine your search results by applying filters such as event genre, specific dates, or locations from the event listing page.
  • Understanding Event Details: Each event page provides comprehensive information including the event's date, time, precise location, ticket price, a detailed description, and an image.

Booking Tickets

  1. Select an Event: Browse through our events and click on the one you're interested in to view its full details.
  2. Choose Quantity: On the event's detail page, select the desired number of tickets you wish to purchase.
  3. Proceed to Payment: Click the "Book Now" or "Proceed to Payment" button to continue.
  4. Payment Process: We use Razorpay for secure and convenient online payments. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete your transaction using your preferred method (cards, net banking, UPI, wallets).
  5. Confirmation: Upon successful payment, you will receive an immediate booking confirmation via email (and optionally SMS). Your new booking will also be visible in your "My Bookings" section.

Viewing Your Bookings

  • Accessing Bookings: Log in to your account and navigate to the "My Bookings" section (typically found in your user dropdown menu or dashboard).
  • Booking Status: Here, you can view details of all your past and upcoming bookings, including the payment status and the quantity of tickets purchased.

For Event Organizers

If you're an organizer looking to host and manage events on our platform, here's how:

  1. Creating a New Event:
    • Log into your account and access the "Create Event" page (usually via a link in your dashboard).
    • Fill in all the required information accurately: Title, detailed Description, Date, Time, Location, Total Tickets available, and Ticket Price.
    • Enhance your event listing by uploading an engaging **Event Image** directly or by providing an **Image URL**.
    • Categorize your event by selecting the appropriate **Genre**, **Audience Type**, and **Event Type** from the dropdowns.
    • Decide whether to **Publish Event** immediately for public viewing or save it as a draft for later.
  2. Managing Your Events:
    • Your organizer dashboard allows you to view and easily edit any events you have created.
